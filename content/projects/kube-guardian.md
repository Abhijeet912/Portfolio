Kube-Guardian is a Kubernetes operations platform built around three pillars:

- **Reliability engineering** — health baselines, SLO tracking and proactive guardrails for clusters.
- **Observability** — unified view of metrics, events and workload state across namespaces.
- **Autonomous incident management** — detecting anomalies and driving remediation workflows with minimal human intervention.

The project is under active development.
