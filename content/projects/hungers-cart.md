Hungers Cart is a complete food delivery service platform, deployed and maintained:

- **Three Android apps** — a customer app for ordering, a partner app for restaurants, and a delivery app for riders.
- **Google Maps API** — live location, routing and delivery tracking.
- **Firebase Authentication** — secure sign-in across all three apps.
